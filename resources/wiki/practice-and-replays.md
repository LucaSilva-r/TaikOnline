Replays let you watch recorded inputs; practice lets you play a selected section repeatedly. Both are learning tools and do not create new ranked results.

## Open a replay

In local single-player Song Select, highlight a song and press **L**. Use Left and Right to select the course and Up and Down to select a play. **Q** switches between the leaderboard and your own plays; Enter watches the selected replay.

Your own list can include failed attempts kept only on this PC. Online replays need a reachable server; the matching chart must be available in your library. The browser is unavailable in local two-player, duet, and Waiwai flows.

## Playback controls

| Default input     | During replay playback             |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Enter or Space    | Pause or resume                    |
| Hold Left / Right | Scrub backwards or forwards        |
| Mouse wheel       | Seek in one-second steps           |
| Up / Down         | Change playback speed              |
| P                 | Practise from the current position |
| Escape            | Open the menu                      |

Button bindings can change these defaults. Read the hints shown above playback when using a custom keyboard or controller setup.

Turning on **Settings → Auto Play** starts songs as a controllable playback session. You can pause, seek, change speed, and enter practice from there too. Auto Play does not save scores.

## Practise a section

Start from a replay or Auto Play session and press **P** at the point you want to practise. In a paused play, use the practice choice in its menu when available. Hit the drum pads to play the section yourself.

Set the beginning with **Q** and the end with **E**. Use **L** to choose what happens at the end: repeat from the beginning of the loop or stop. Left and Right scrub, and **Tab + Left / Right** jump between chart notes. Up and Down adjust speed. Enter or Space pauses and resumes.

When practising live, drum pads are inputs to the song. Pause before using controls that would otherwise act as hits on your particular bindings. Use the on-screen hints to confirm the current loop and playback state.

## Layouts and recorded plays

A replay uses the viewer's own active layout. It does not reproduce the other player's personal layout preset. Changing playback speed, seeking, or practising does not overwrite the original recorded score.

Opening either editor makes that session unranked as well. To save a fresh normal play, leave practice or playback and start or restart the song normally.
