TaikOnline gives Waddamburo player accounts, score uploads, rankings, and online rooms. You can still play locally as Guest without signing in.

## Sign in at home

1. Create or sign in to your TaikOnline account and accept the current Rules.
2. On Waddamburo's player-select screen, choose **Sign in**.
3. Scan the QR code, or open **Play** on TaikOnline and enter the code shown by the game.
4. Return to Waddamburo, select your account, and start playing.

The account is remembered on that PC. For a visitor on someone else's signed-in installation, choose **Join with code** so the visitor's login is kept in memory rather than saved there. Each side in local two-player play can use its own account.

Arcade cabinets use a separate card-pairing workflow and operator-issued credentials. Follow [Home and Arcade modes](home-and-arcade) for cabinet login.

## Scores and rankings

In local single-player Song Select, highlight a song and press **L** to open its score browser. Left and Right select a course; Up and Down select a play. **Q** switches between the leaderboard and your own plays. **Tab** switches the normal and Shinuchi leaderboards when viewing rankings. Enter opens a selected replay.

Guest plays are local. Failed plays also stay on the PC and do not receive an online rank or crown. Replays, practice, Auto Play, and a run that enters an editor do not save a new ranked result. Closing the editor does not restore that run's eligibility: restart normally to record another play.

Eligible plays recorded offline for a remembered account can upload after reconnecting. If results are missing, check the selected account, connection status, notices, and Rules acceptance before assuming they were lost.

## Play with another person online

Rooms are available in **Home mode with one local player signed in and a server configured**. Local two-player mode and Arcade do not open the online lobby.

1. In Song Select, open the **Online** spine.
2. Read the connection notice. Matches can connect directly to the other player, who can see your public IP address; this also applies to strangers in Quick Play and public rooms.
3. Choose **Quick Play**, **Host a room**, **Rooms**, or **Join with a code**.
4. Once connected, the designated player chooses a song. Each player selects a course available in their copy, then both games load before play begins.

The host can choose public or private visibility, set a password, and choose whether the host, guest, or alternating players select songs. Share the room code privately when you want a specific person to join. A private room is hidden from the public room list.

The room banner tells you whose turn it is and whether the game is waiting for a choice, a connection, or loading. Use **Leave room** when finished; simply closing the lobby panel can leave you in the room.

## Songs in an online room

Each player needs a playable copy of the selected official song and course. Waddamburo does not send original game libraries to a partner who lacks them.

For supported custom songs, Waddamburo can send the chart and audio when the other player does not have them. The first time, it asks you to confirm that you are authorised to share those files. The received song stays in the other game's memory. Refusing sharing, an unreadable or oversized song, or a missing course can prevent that selection from starting.

Layout and modchart editing are unavailable during an online match. Prepare changes during a local session. See [Layout editor](layout-editor) for how layouts and authored effects interact.

## Connection problems

If you cannot reach the server, check your internet connection and the server setting. If a room is missing, it may have closed or filled; ask the host for a current code. If a password is rejected, re-enter it exactly.

If the server works but a match cannot connect, try leaving and rejoining once and check both games are updated. Include **waddamburo/logs/latest.log** when reporting a repeatable connection failure. For other issues, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting).
