You must be at least [MINIMUM AGE] years old to have an account.

## Fair play

- Play with your own inputs. No macros, autoplay, input scripts, roll or balloon assists, or anything that plays or drums for you.
- Do not modify the client, replays or uploads to change results, and do not submit fake or altered scores.
- Do not upload other players' replays as your own.
- Do not upload malformed or malicious data, or try to break, overload or get around the service.
- Do not use another account to get around a sanction.

## Accounts and behaviour

- Use one account per person, and do not impersonate other people.
- Names, titles and profile pictures must not be offensive, hateful, sexual or harassing.
- Do not harass other players.
- Report honestly: repeated reports made in bad faith can get you blocked from reporting.

## Respect the games and their owners

Do not upload anything you have no right to share. Do not use the service to distribute game files, keys or other copyrighted material.

## How the Rules are enforced

Scores are recalculated by the server from your replay. Plays are checked by automated tools and by staff, and staff decide what to do. Breaking the Rules can get plays hidden, limit where your plays are shown, or get your account restricted or banned, in some cases without advance notice.

Hardware problems can look like cheating. If you think a decision is a mistake, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

If you delete your account while banned, a scrambled form of your email address is kept until the ban would have ended, so it cannot be used to sign up again.

## The service itself

The service is in early development and offered as it is. It can change, go down or end at any time, and data can be lost or reset. These Rules can change: when a change matters, you will be asked to accept them again.
